# 11.2 — Domain & Data (fixture)

## Purpose & Scope

Owns the entity register (`ENTITY-###`). Each entity names its identity and its
invariants.

This bar surfaced building a synthetic planning service, where an unstated
invariant let two rows share an identity.

## Design Decisions

We chose per-entity invariants over a global constraint table.
